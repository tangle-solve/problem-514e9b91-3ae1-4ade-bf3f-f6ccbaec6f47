### CLIM-000034: Three-dimensional solid-Earth feedback on Antarctic retreat
- **Subfield:** Ice sheets, glaciers, and sea ice
- **Open:** Infer mantle viscosity and lithospheric structure sufficiently to jointly predict unloading, uplift, gravitational changes, water redistribution, and ice retreat.
- **Status:** Gomez et al. (2024) showed realistic three-dimensional structure materially changes projected sea levels; that structure remains incompletely observed.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Rebound affects both ice stability and regional sea-level fingerprints.
- **Key works:** Gomez et al. (2024), [“The influence of realistic 3D mantle viscosity on Antarctica’s contribution to future global sea levels”](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn1470).
- **Related:** Seismic inverse problems; mantle viscoelastic rheology.
